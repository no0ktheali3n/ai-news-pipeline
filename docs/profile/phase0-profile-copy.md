Positioning (from the content-engine spec): **practitioner-curator** in the applied-AI lane.
The promise a visitor should get in 3 seconds: *"a working AI engineer picks the one paper
a day that should change what builders do — and the picking is itself a live AI system."*

Everything below is paste-ready; `[...]` bits are yours to fill.

## Display name

Keep your name/handle primary; add the lane as a suffix so search and skims catch it:

- `[Name] — AI systems & agents`
- `[Name] | applied AI, daily papers`

Avoid "AI enthusiast", "🤖", "opinions my own" — enthusiast-signals repel the practitioner audience.

## Bio (X limit: 160 chars — all three options fit)

**Option A — the promise, straight (recommended):**
> AI engineer (R&D). One arXiv paper a weekday that changes what you build — agents, LLM systems, AI security. Picked & posted by a pipeline I built.

**Option B — the system is the flex:**
> My pipeline reads arXiv every morning, scores 40 papers on builder-relevance, and posts the winner. I built it; I work on production AI systems for a living.

**Option C — minimal/dry:**
> Daily builder-relevant signal from arXiv: agents · LLM systems · AI security. Human engineer, automated taste. Code in the pinned thread.

Why A: it leads with *who* (credibility), then *what you get* (the promise), then the twist
(*the curator is a pipeline*) — which is the account's only truly unique hook.
